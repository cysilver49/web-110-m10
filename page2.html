Visit These Parks and Attractions

Municipal Park

A family friendly playground and a seasonal splash pad.

Clemmons Educational State Forest

An 800 acre forest featuring walking and hiking trails, educational "talking trees," camping, and picnic areas.

The Clayton Center

Located in a historic schoolhouse downtown, hosting live musical acts, tribute shows, and performing arts.

Clayton Sculpture Trail

An open-air downtown art walk featuring rotating metal and mixed-media sculptures.
